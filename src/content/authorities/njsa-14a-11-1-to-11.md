Chapter 11 provides New Jersey's procedural appraisal remedy. It identifies the corporate actions that trigger dissenters' rights, prescribes notice and demand requirements, and provides for an offer by the corporation followed, when necessary, by a court proceeding to determine fair value.

The statutory phrase does not resolve every valuation issue by itself. New Jersey decisions, including *Lawson Mardon Wheaton, Inc. v. Smith*, explain that fair value protects the dissenter's proportionate interest in the enterprise and ordinarily does not permit a marketability discount that would transfer value to the majority. The statutory procedure and the judicial interpretation should therefore be read together.
