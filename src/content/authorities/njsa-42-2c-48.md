Section 42:2C-48 governs judicial dissolution and alternative relief for New Jersey limited liability companies. On application by a member, the statutory grounds include unlawful operations, impracticability of carrying on the company's activities in conformity with its formation documents, and conduct by controlling members or managers that is illegal, fraudulent, or oppressive and directly harmful to the applicant.

The court may order a remedy other than dissolution when that remedy is fair and equitable, including the sale of the applicant's interest to the company or other members. Unlike the corporate buyout provision, the LLC section does not provide a detailed valuation formula or an automatic election to purchase. The operating agreement, the nature of the wrong, and the court's equitable remedial choice remain important to any valuation analysis.
