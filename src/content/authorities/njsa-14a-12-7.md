Section 14A:12-7 authorizes judicial intervention in specified disputes involving New Jersey corporations. For corporations with twenty-five or fewer shareholders, the statutory grounds include fraudulent or illegal conduct, abuse of authority, waste or misapplication of corporate assets, and conduct that is oppressive or unfair toward one or more minority shareholders in their capacity as shareholders, directors, officers, or employees.

Subsection (8) allows the court, upon a party's motion, to order the corporation or one or more shareholders to purchase another shareholder's stock at fair value. The presumptive valuation date is the commencement of the action, although the court may select another date or make equitable adjustments where oppression caused or threatened a material change in value. The statute creates an equitable remedy; the cases determine how fair value and discounts operate on particular facts.
