Section 262 provides Delaware's statutory appraisal remedy and specifies eligibility, notice, demand, filing, discovery, valuation, interest, and payment procedures. Under subsection (h), the Court of Chancery determines the fair value of the shares, exclusive of any element of value arising from the accomplishment or expectation of the merger or consolidation, while taking into account all relevant factors.

Delaware decisions describe the object as the corporation's value as a going concern to the stockholder, not its buyer-specific acquisition value. Market evidence can be highly probative, including an arm's-length deal price, but merger-created synergies must be excluded. *M.P.M. Enterprises* and *Verition Partners v. Aruba Networks* illustrate both sides of that rule.
